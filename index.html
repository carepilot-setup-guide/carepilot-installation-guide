1. About iClinic CarePilot

iClinic CarePilot is an AI-powered clinical documentation solution integrated directly into the iClinic office visit workflow. It combines ambient clinical documentation, core ICD-10 and CPT coding suggestions, and Pre-Visit Planning to help providers prepare for visits, reduce documentation effort, and spend more time focused on patient care.

CarePilot Scribe is the ambient documentation capability within CarePilot. It captures the natural conversation during the patient encounter and generates structured draft clinical documentation for provider review. As part of the ambient workflow, CarePilot can also provide suggested ICD-10 diagnosis and CPT codes based on the documented encounter. The provider reviews and confirms the documentation and coding suggestions before sending the applicable information to iClinic.

CarePilot Pre-Visit Planning reviews available patient information before the encounter and surfaces relevant clinical information that may help the provider prepare for the visit. Depending on the information available in the patient's chart, this may include chronic conditions, current medications, previously documented issues, prior diagnostics, suspected conditions, and potential care gaps that may warrant the provider's attention.

DocPro is MDLand's technology partner supporting the CarePilot experience. CarePilot is offered and integrated within the iClinic environment, with applicable functionality identified as "Powered by DocPro." MDLand remains the primary point of contact for CarePilot customers.

2. Features & How It Works

CarePilot is integrated into the iClinic office visit workflow. For an activated provider, CarePilot can capture the natural patient-provider conversation during the encounter and generate structured draft clinical documentation. The provider remains in control of the clinical record and reviews the generated content before sending it to iClinic.

  • Chief Complaint / Reason for Visit
  • History of Present Illness (HPI)
  • Review of Systems
  • Physical Examination findings
  • Assessment & Plan
  • Suggested ICD-10 diagnosis codes
  • Suggested CPT codes

Yes. Core ICD-10 and CPT code suggestions generated as part of the ambient documentation workflow are included with the standard CarePilot subscription. These suggestions are intended to assist the provider and should be reviewed for accuracy and appropriateness before being accepted or incorporated into the patient's record.

Providers remain in control of their documentation. CarePilot-generated content can be reviewed and incorporated into the clinical note as appropriate rather than automatically replacing the provider's documentation. Depending on the workflow, providers can determine which CarePilot-generated sections should be incorporated into the final note.

After the encounter, CarePilot generates draft documentation for the provider to review. Once reviewed, the applicable content can be sent into the corresponding sections of the iClinic Office Visit. Providers should review and make any necessary corrections before finalizing the clinical documentation.

CarePilot supports multiple languages and is designed to accommodate a broad range of patient-provider conversations. Specific language and dialect support may vary. Practices with specific language requirements should contact MDLand for additional information.

3. Mobile & Device Access

Yes. CarePilot supports a mobile workflow for providers who want to use ambient documentation from a supported mobile device during the patient encounter.

Mobile access requires a CarePilot account to be set up for the provider using the provider's email address. Once the account is created and activated, the provider uses that email address to log in to the CarePilot mobile experience.

Yes. Because CarePilot subscriptions and clinical activity are provider-specific, the mobile account should be established for the individual participating provider using that provider's email address.

MDLand will coordinate the mobile account setup. At minimum, the participating provider's identifying information and email address are required so the CarePilot account can be created and associated with the appropriate provider.

CarePilot can be used on supported desktop and mobile devices with the required microphone access for ambient documentation. Device, browser, and operating-system requirements may vary. MDLand can provide the current supported-device requirements during onboarding.
